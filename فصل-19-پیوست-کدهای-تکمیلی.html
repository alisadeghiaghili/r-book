19.2 کدهای تکمیلی از Rhistory

کد: summarize / group_by (4)

iris |>
  group_by(Species) |>
  summarize(msl = mean(Sepal.Length),
            stdtl = sd(Sepal.Length),
            count = n())
# A tibble: 3 × 4
  Species      msl stdtl count

2 versicolor  5.94 0.516    50
